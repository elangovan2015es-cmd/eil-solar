📐 How Self Consumption Ratio is Calculated
What is Self Consumption Ratio (SCR)?
SCR = Units actually used from solar ÷ Total solar generated.
The higher the SCR, the more of your solar you are using at full value.
🏠 Residential — Why 98%?
Residential connections use Net Metering (LT I-A / LA1A).
Any solar you don't use immediately is exported to TANGEDCO grid.
TANGEDCO credits those units 1:1 back to your account.
You use those credits at night / morning → effectively 100% utilised.
We use 98% (not 100%) to account for minor metering and wiring losses.
🏢 Commercial / Industrial — Why 75%?
Commercial connections use Feed-in Tariff (FiT) (LM51 / HT).
Units self-consumed save you the full tariff (₹10.45 or ₹7.50/kWh).
Units exported earn only the FiT rate: ₹3.61/kWh — much less.
We assume 75% is consumed directly, 25% exported at the low FiT rate.
This is conservative — actual SC depends on shift timings vs solar hours.
📊 DUF Formula — Demand Utilization Factor
When you enter Sanctioned Load and Recorded Demand from the bill:
DUF = Recorded Demand ÷ Sanctioned Load
Adjusted SC = MIN(DUF × 6.5, 98%)

The 6.5 factor = peak solar hours per day when generation is usable.
If DUF × 6.5 exceeds 98%, net metering caps it at 98% anyway.

Example 1 — Bill #46: 1 kW ÷ 7 kW = 14.3% → SC = MIN(0.143 × 6.5, 98%) = 92.9%
Example 2 — Elango's house: 3 kW ÷ 7 kW = 42.9% → SC = MIN(0.429 × 6.5, 98%) = 98% ✅
(Because 0.429 × 6.5 = 2.79 which exceeds 0.98 → capped at 98%)
🔢 System Size Recommendation Formula
Step 1: Monthly units = Bi-monthly units ÷ 2
Step 2: Annual units = Monthly × 12
Step 3: Recommended kWp = Annual units ÷ Generation Factor (1,600 for Chennai)
Step 4: Cap at Sanctioned Load if lower

Elango's house example:
Bill #46: 946 bi-monthly → 473/month → 5,676/year
5,676 ÷ 1,600 = 3.5 kWp recommended
Sanctioned load 7 kW → no constraint → 3.5 kWp ✅
At 3 kW recorded demand → will self-consume most generation → excellent solar candidate
